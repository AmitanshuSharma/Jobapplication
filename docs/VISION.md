# Vision

## Mission

Build a personal, local-first Job Intelligence Platform that automatically crawls targeted company career pages, scores Data Engineering roles against a personalized relevance model, and delivers timely alerts — so a senior engineer spends time evaluating opportunities, not hunting for them.

## Problem Statement

Strong engineers waste hours manually checking dozens of career pages, filtering irrelevant roles, and losing track of where they stand in each process. No existing tool combines personalized crawling, weighted scoring, alerting, and pipeline tracking in a single local-first system built for one person's exact criteria.

## Target User

A senior Data Engineer with 4.5+ years of experience across Databricks, PySpark, SQL, DBT, Airflow, Snowflake, and AWS/Azure. Transitioning from consulting and pre-sales-heavy work into hands-on technical leadership at product companies. Targeting Senior, Lead, Staff, or Platform Data Engineer roles. Preferred locations: Gurugram, Noida, Pune, Hyderabad, Remote, or Hybrid. Desired compensation around 30 LPA.

## Success Criteria

- All target company career pages crawled daily without manual effort.
- Only high-scoring matches (score >= 20) surface in alerts and the dashboard.
- Telegram and Email notifications fire on new matches with no duplicate alerts.
- Dashboard gives clear visibility into job pipeline status, tags, and referral contacts.
- Every job record includes referral tracking fields.
- System operates fully on a local machine without requiring paid cloud infrastructure.
- Target companies are prioritized according to predefined company rankings.
## Non-Goals

- Not a mass-apply or auto-apply tool.
- Not a multi-user platform or SaaS product.
- Not a CRM for managing recruiters broadly.
- Not a resume builder, cover letter generator, or LinkedIn automation tool.
- Roles outside Data Engineering are explicitly penalized in scoring, not surfaced.
